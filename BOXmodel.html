Abes Engineering College

ABES Engineering College (ABESEC) is a well-known private engineering institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. Established in 2000, it is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and approved by AICTE. The college is recognized for its strong academic environment, modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and emphasis on technical as well as professional development. ABES offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, computer applications, and management, and it actively promotes research, innovation, and industry collaboration. The campus also supports students through placement training, extracurricular activities, and skill-development initiatives, helping them prepare for successful careers.
